Personal Brand Story

Empowering Organizations to Articulate Their Worth and Win Sustainable Funding.

Mirelle Lucien is an experienced Grant Writer, Grant Researcher & Proposal Writer, Grant Consultant, and Nonprofit Funding Specialist. Her consulting practice was founded on a simple truth: countless organizations perform life-changing work every day, yet struggle to secure the institutional capital they need because their proposals fail to meet the stringent technical expectations of modern grantmakers.

With a multidisciplinary background blending analytical research with persuasive narrative development, Mirelle translates intricate programs into clear, evidence-based grant proposals. She dissects complex Request for Proposals (RFPs), designs robust Theories of Change, aligns budget expenditures with measurable impact indicators, and ensures total compliance with funder scoring rubrics.

Whether working with a high-growth social enterprise in London, an environmental conservation initiative in East Africa, or a community arts organization in North America, Mirelle brings unwavering dedication, strategic rigor, and an empathetic approach to every application.

Our Vision

Guiding Principles

The 5 Core Values Behind Every Engagement

Every proposal, research dossier, and consulting session is held to rigorous standards designed to maximize trust and funding success.

Accuracy

Meticulous adherence to funder guidelines, eligible expenditures, verified statistics, and technical application formatting.

Integrity

Honest assessments of funding feasibility, clear communication of timelines, and absolute client data confidentiality.

Purpose

Centering your mission’s authentic heartbeat in every sentence so reviewers connect with the human stakes of your work.

Strategy

Positioning applications against funder strategic priorities, distinct institutional benchmarks, and peer differentiators.

Impact

Proving measurable outcomes, tangible return on donor investment, and durable community self-sufficiency.

End-to-End Progression

The 5-Stage Funding Readiness Pathway

From the first opportunity search to submission-ready collateral, here is how we systematically build funding success.

STAGE 01

Research

Comprehensive database mapping, RFP alignment audits, and giving behavior analysis.

STAGE 02

Strategy

Program logic modeling, target budget framing, and stakeholder value proposition synthesis.

STAGE 03

Writing

Drafting clear, compelling narratives, executive summaries, and required technical annexes.

STAGE 04

Refinement

Peer review against funder rubrics, budget consistency cross-checks, and editorial polish.

STAGE 05

Funding Readiness

Final submission assembly, portal compliance verification, and post-submission briefing.
