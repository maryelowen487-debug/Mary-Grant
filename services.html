Service 01

Grant Writer

Professional grant narratives aligned with specific funding requirements and organizational goals. Mirelle crafts captivating, compliant application narratives that directly address review criteria while maintaining the authentic passion of your mission.

Key Deliverables:

  • Complete application drafting (Letters of Intent, full narrative applications)
  • Statements of Need supported by empirical citations & community data
  • Budget narrative justification aligned with project timeline expenditures
  • Comprehensive compliance and rubric alignment review

Service 02

Grant Research

Research relevant opportunities, eligibility criteria, submission deadlines, and potential funding alignment. Eliminate the guesswork and prevent wasted effort by targeting funders with a proven track record of investing in your thematic and geographic space.

Key Deliverables:

  • Curated Funder Matrix (foundations, corporate philanthropies, government tenders)
  • Deep-dive eligibility audits and historical award amount benchmarks
  • Strategic Alignment Scoring to prioritize high-probability opportunities
  • Annual grant calendar with structured deadlines and lead-time reminders

Service 03

Proposal Writer

Develop compelling and structured proposals that clearly communicate the project, need, objectives, activities, methodology, and expected impact. Every section is written to present a bulletproof case for why your organization is the ideal steward of funding.

Key Deliverables:

  • Theories of Change, Logical Frameworks (Logframes), and Work Breakdown Structures
  • Monitoring, Evaluation, and Learning (MEL) plans with actionable KPIs
  • Organizational capability profiles and project leadership qualifications
  • Risk mitigation, sustainability, and post-grant continuity models

Grant Consultant

Provide strategic guidance on funding readiness, opportunity fit, application planning, and proposal improvement. Ideal for organizations that want high-level review of past unsuccessful proposals or institutional capacity building for in-house teams.
